# Non-Invincible Stealth Bombers
This mod removes the Stealth Bomber monopoly in the endgame, giving them 15% of normal interception damage instead of 0%.

Made to work with Civ V - Gods & Kings (included with Unciv), but if you play with Vanilla, open an issue and ping me by writing @BobbyCobby at the beginning. I'm happy to do it but haven't bothered because I use Gods & Kings.

To test, load test_save. You're China. Attack Barcelona with your Stealth Bomber and see what happens.
